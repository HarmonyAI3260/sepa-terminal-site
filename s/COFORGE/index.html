SEPA Composite (custom)

Percentile (1–99) of 0.35×RS Rating + 0.25×EPS Rating + 0.15×A/D percentile + 0.10×(100 − group-rank percentile) + 0.15×trend points, where trend points = 100×Trend-Template passed/8 in Stage 2 and half of that otherwise. Missing components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the score needs an RS Rating and at least one of the EPS or A/D ratings.

Inputs used: ad, eps, group, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

Weights: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Effective weights on this row: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Basis: full. Missing-input policy: absent components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the row is ranked in the same cohort as full-input rows.
