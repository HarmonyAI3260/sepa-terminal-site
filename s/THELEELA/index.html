Definitions — how every rating on this page is computed

Composite Score

Percentile (1–99) of 0.35×RS Rating + 0.25×EPS Rating + 0.15×A/D percentile + 0.10×(100 − group-rank percentile) + 0.15×trend points, where trend points = 100×Trend-Template passed/8 in Stage 2 and half of that otherwise. Missing components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the score needs an RS Rating and at least one of the EPS or A/D ratings.

Inputs used: ad, eps, group, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

EPS Rating

Percentile (1–99) of 0.5×(mean EPS YoY growth of the last two reported quarters) + 0.5×(3-year annual EPS CAGR), each winsorised at ±300 %. PAT growth substitutes for a missing quarterly EPS and the substitution is flagged.

Inputs used: Mar 2026 EPS YoY, Jun 2026 EPS YoY, Mar 2023 EPS -1.85, Mar 2026 EPS 12.07. Coverage: complete for this row.

RS Rating (Price Strength)

The scan's universe relative-strength percentile: the weighted absolute-return blend 0.4×3m + 0.2×6m + 0.4×12m ranked across every non-stale scanned symbol.

Inputs used: universe RS percentile. Coverage: complete for this row.

A/D Rating

13 weeks (65 sessions) of money flow: Σ volume×((close−low)−(high−close))/(high−low) ÷ Σ volume, ranked across the scan and lettered A+ … E. Needs 30 sessions.

Inputs used: 65 sessions of money flow. Coverage: complete for this row.

Group Rank

Groups are NSE basic-industry classifications with at least three scanned members, ranked 1..N by median member RS percentile.

Inputs used: median member RS percentile. Coverage: complete for this row.

EPS Growth Rate

Three-year compound annual growth rate of annual EPS (fiscal years to March).

Inputs used: Mar 2023 EPS -1.85, Mar 2026 EPS 12.07. Coverage: Mar 2023 EPS is not positive, so a CAGR is undefined.

Earnings Stability

Percentile (1–99) of the inverse coefficient of variation of the last 12 quarters' EPS YoY growth: higher means steadier. Needs 8 quarters.

Inputs used: 3 quarters of EPS YoY growth. Coverage: only 3 quarters of YoY growth (8 required).

Meter words: POOR < 40 · FAIR 40–69 · GOOD 70–84 · GREAT ≥ 85. A/D letters run A+ (≥ 90th percentile) to E (< 10th). These are this project's own percentile ratings over its own scanned universe; they are not IBD, O'Neil or MarketSmith ratings and no value is copied from any other product.
