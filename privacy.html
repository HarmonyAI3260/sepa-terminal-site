Privacy policy

Effective date: 5 September 2026.

What this app does

SEPA Terminal publishes static research snapshots of NSE stocks. You can browse without an account. There are no accounts, analytics, tracking cookies, advertising identifiers or tracking scripts. We do not collect or share your personal data.

What stays on your device

The only user-specific settings stored are your own filter preferences and, for the owner, the refresh-control preference and a refresh key in browser storage on the owner's refresh console. The public site does not receive that key. Preferences are not sent to us or synced between devices.

For offline reading, your browser also caches public app files and previously loaded research snapshots. These are copies of public content, not personal records. Cached figures can be out of date; check the snapshot timestamp. Clear this site's storage in your browser or app settings to remove local preferences and cached pages. The owner can separately clear storage for the refresh console to remove its key.

Hosting and other websites

GitHub Pages delivers this static site. Hosting providers receive ordinary web requests and may retain technical access logs under their own policies. Fonts and the chart library are loaded from third-party providers; those requests expose standard connection details such as your IP address. SEPA Terminal adds no analytics or tracking to those requests.

Links to TradingView, screener.in and other sources open independent services. Their privacy policies apply when you use them.

Research only

This is an educational research aid, not investment advice or a recommendation to buy or sell. No orders are placed. Verify information against official exchange filings.
