Missing-input policy: absent components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the row is ranked in the same cohort as full-input rows. Comparability: partial and full-input composites share one percentile cohort and are not comparable to any commercial rating; the full-evidence composite is the like-for-like figure.
