Concall highlights

  • Per the Jul 2026 concall, Q1 FY27 produced the company's highest-ever quarterly revenue, profitability and order book.
  • Management raised its FY27 revenue-growth aspiration from around 20% to 40% or more and expects the improved margin level to continue, barring external geopolitical or logistics shocks.
  • The approximately ₹26,665 crore order book was described as five times FY26 revenue.
  • Management expects exports to represent roughly 60-65% of revenue, with domestic business contributing the balance.
  • Data-centre connectivity is expected to generate more than ₹700 crore in FY27; closing remarks indicated an expectation of about ₹800 crore.
  • Optical-fibre capacity is scheduled to increase to 34 million fibre-km by Dec 2026, while cable capacity is planned to increase to 43 million fibre-km.
  • Management expects higher customer pricing and a richer mix of value-added data-centre products to offset higher preform costs.
  • HFCL remains on track for approximately ₹500 crore of FY27 defence revenue, per management.
  • Telecom products are expected to contribute ₹500-600 crore for FY27, with management indicating EBITDA margins of roughly 12-15% for that category.
