SEPA Composite (custom)

Missing components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the score needs an RS Rating and at least one of the EPS or A/D ratings.

Inputs used: ad, eps, group, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

Weights: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Effective weights on this row: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Basis: full. Missing-input policy: absent components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the row is ranked in the same cohort as full-input rows. Comparability: partial and full-input composites share one percentile cohort and are not comparable to any commercial rating; the full-evidence composite is the like-for-like figure.
