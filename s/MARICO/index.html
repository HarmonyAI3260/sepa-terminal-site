WATCHLIST

Marico is tightening into a third VCP contraction with improving earnings optics, but Stage 1 trend status, a volume-dry-up miss, a pivot that isn't near the base highs, and a sub-200d-index backdrop mean this stays a watch, not a buy, until the Trend Template clears and a qualified pattern actually triggers.

4/10

Guidance / runway

  • TTM sales growth (25.1%) is outrunning TTM PAT growth (15.0%), so the trailing-year profit engine hasn't fully caught up to the revenue acceleration. Five-year sales CAGR is only ~11% per Screener, so this quarter's inflection needs more prints before it's trusted as durable rather than a high-base-adjacent pop. The underlying growth engines are diversifying well: Foods +43% YoY crossing ₹1,300cr ARR, digital-first brands (Beardo/Plix) at >₹1,100cr ARR and scaling profitably, and the flagship Parachute Rigids posting its strongest volume growth in 20 quarters.
  • FY27 guidance: double-digit revenue past ₹15,000cr, high-teens-to-20% EBITDA growth
  • Q2 FY27 pre-announcement: management optimistic of surpassing near-term guidance, margin improvement flagged

SEPA Composite (custom)

Percentile (1–99) of 0.35×RS Rating + 0.25×EPS Rating + 0.15×A/D percentile + 0.10×(100 − group-rank percentile) + 0.15×trend points, where trend points = 100×Trend-Template passed/8 in Stage 2 and half of that otherwise. Missing components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the score needs an RS Rating and at least one of the EPS or A/D ratings.

Inputs used: ad, eps, group, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

Weights: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Effective weights on this row: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Basis: full. Missing-input policy: absent components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the row is ranked in the same cohort as full-input rows.
