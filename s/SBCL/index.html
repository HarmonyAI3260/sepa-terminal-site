Concall highlights

  • Management expects FY27 consolidated revenue growth of 20-30%, subject to customer forecasts and execution.
  • Bus-bar and cell-connecting revenue was minimal in Q1FY27; production has begun, with a larger contribution expected from current and upcoming quarters and the main Pune facility expected to be fully operational in October.
  • Assemblies, including PCB and bus-bar assemblies, could contribute approximately 15-16% of first-year consolidated revenue.
  • Shunt strip sales fell to roughly one-third of the prior-year level as lower-value strip was converted into higher-value finished parts.
  • Approximately half of the YoY contacts revenue growth was attributed to higher silver prices; nevertheless, sequential growth occurred despite silver prices being flat to lower.
  • Growth demand is strongest in two-wheeler EVs and smart meters, with four-wheeler cell-connecting opportunities also under development.
  • Largest-customer concentration is expected to remain below approximately 17-18%, versus a historical peak near 35-40%.
  • Shunt welding capacity utilization is approximately 65-70% and can be expanded within two to three months; bimetal utilization is approximately 40-45%.
  • Management highlighted Asia as weak and identified margin quality, working-capital efficiency and cash conversion as priorities.
