Definitions — how every rating on this page is computed

SEPA Composite (custom)

Percentile (1–99) of 0.35×RS Rating + 0.25×EPS Rating + 0.15×A/D percentile + 0.10×(100 − group-rank percentile) + 0.15×trend points, where trend points = 100×Trend-Template passed/8 in Stage 2 and half of that otherwise. Missing components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the score needs an RS Rating and at least one of the EPS or A/D ratings.

Inputs used: ad, eps, group, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

Version: sepa-ratings-1.1. Cohort ranked: 2,019 rows. Rank method: strict-lower empirical percentile, ceil, capped 1–99; ties share a percentile. Weights: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Effective weights on this row: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Basis: full. Missing-input policy: absent components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the row is ranked in the same cohort as full-input rows. Comparability: partial and full-input composites share one percentile cohort and are not comparable to any commercial rating; the full-evidence composite is the like-for-like figure.

SEPA Composite (full-evidence)

The same weighted mean as the Composite, ranked (1–99) only within the cohort of rows that carry all five components (RS, EPS, A/D, group and trend). A row missing any component is not ranked at all, so this figure is the like-for-like one.

Inputs used: ad, eps, group, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

Version: sepa-ratings-1.1. Cohort ranked: 298 rows. Rank method: strict-lower empirical percentile, ceil, capped 1–99; ties share a percentile. Weights: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Basis: full. Missing-input policy: a row missing any component is not ranked here at all. Comparability: partial and full-input composites share one percentile cohort and are not comparable to any commercial rating; the full-evidence composite is the like-for-like figure.

SEPA EPS score

Percentile (1–99) of 0.5×(mean EPS YoY growth of the last two reported quarters) + 0.5×(3-year annual EPS CAGR), each winsorised at ±300 %. PAT growth substitutes for a missing quarterly EPS and the substitution is flagged.

Inputs used: Mar 2026 EPS YoY, Jun 2026 EPS YoY. Coverage: complete for this row.

Version: sepa-ratings-1.1.

SEPA RS percentile

Inputs used: universe RS percentile. Coverage: complete for this row.

Version: sepa-ratings-1.1.
