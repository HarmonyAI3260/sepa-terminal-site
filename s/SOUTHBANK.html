VERDICT

STRONG CANDIDATE

A high-quality, volume-confirmed Stage 2 VCP breakout inside the buy zone, supported by institutional accumulation and improving bank metrics, but capped by sub-Code-33 growth and a hostile Nifty 50 regime.

7/10

Guidance / runway

  • The operating direction is constructive rather than explosive. Q1 FY27 advances grew 17%, deposits 11%, retail deposits 14%, CASA 15%, NII 23% and PAT 17% per concall. Gold loans grew 43%, mortgages 34%, home loans 19% and auto loans 34%. However, TTM reported sales growth is only 6.9% and TTM profit growth 13.5%; Jun 2026 EPS also fell 7.7% sequentially after a strong Mar quarter. This is an improving bank turnaround, not a classic 30%-plus earnings accelerator.

Catalysts & concall

  • NIM recovery and record quarterly NIIQ1 FY27 concall, July 2026NIM recovered to 3.23% and NII rose 23% to a record ₹1,025 crore. Deposit repricing, CASA growth and lower bulk deposits have repaired the core spread trajectory.
  • Higher-yielding retail and MSME asset-mix shiftQ1 FY27 concall, July 2026Management plans to deploy capital into retail and MSME assets and expects ROA to migrate over time from roughly 1.00%-1.15% toward 1.20%-1.25%, although the outgoing CEO declined to bind his successor to firm guidance.
  • Trade and FX platform rolloutQ1 FY27 concall, July 2026Management expected the upgraded trade and FX platform to go live by the end of September, potentially supporting fee recovery and broader product participation.
  • RBI approval for incoming MD and CEOET BFSI news headline, July 8, 2026RBI approval of Mahesh Murlidhar Pai for a three-year term reduces succession uncertainty, though execution continuity must still be demonstrated.
  • Mutual-fund ownership crosses 5%ET BFSI news headline, July 24, 2026Kotak Mahindra Mutual Fund raising its stake beyond 5% reinforces the dossier's broader evidence of rising institutional sponsorship.

Concall highlights

  • Q1 FY27 PAT was ₹378 crore, up 17% YoY; reported Jun 2026 EPS growth was 17.1%.
  • Deposits grew 11%, retail deposits 14%, advances 17%, total business 14% and CASA 15% YoY.
  • NIM reached 3.23%, up 28 basis points QoQ and 20 basis points YoY; management expects positive full-year outcomes but provided no numerical NIM guidance.
  • Higher-cost deposits repriced downward by 40-60 basis points and bulk deposits were cut approximately 50%; management said most of this repricing benefit is already baked in.
  • GNPA declined to 1.38%, NNPA to 0.26%, provision coverage including write-offs rose to 94.51%, and quarterly credit cost was 9 basis points.
  • Management expects operating expenses to rise roughly 5%-6% and is targeting positive operating leverage for the full year.
  • Management intends to grow the balance sheet around market growth plus approximately two percentage points while shifting toward higher-yielding retail and MSME assets.
  • MSME growth was described as below expectation, while management plans to return to scale mode after a cautious quarter.
  • legacy 3Q consistency test failure: no three-quarter sequence of at least 20% EPS and reported sales growth.
  • The Nifty 50 is in a correction below its 200-day average; the Nifty 500 is above its 200-day but below its 50-day, creating a low-forgiveness breakout environment.
  • Three-month relative performance is marginally behind the Nifty 500 despite strong six- and twelve-month leadership.
  • A substantial part of the deposit-repricing benefit is already reflected, reducing the probability of another comparable NIM step-up.
  • Leadership transition creates execution risk around asset-mix change, fee recovery, branch expansion and prior profitability objectives.
