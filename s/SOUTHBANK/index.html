Concall highlights

  • Q1 FY27 PAT was ₹378 crore, up 17% YoY; reported Jun 2026 EPS growth was 17.1%.
  • Deposits grew 11%, retail deposits 14%, advances 17%, total business 14% and CASA 15% YoY.
  • NIM reached 3.23%, up 28 basis points QoQ and 20 basis points YoY; management expects positive full-year outcomes but provided no numerical NIM guidance.
  • Higher-cost deposits repriced downward by 40-60 basis points and bulk deposits were cut approximately 50%; management said most of this repricing benefit is already baked in.
  • GNPA declined to 1.38%, NNPA to 0.26%, provision coverage including write-offs rose to 94.51%, and quarterly credit cost was 9 basis points.
  • Management expects operating expenses to rise roughly 5%-6% and is targeting positive operating leverage for the full year.
  • Management intends to grow the balance sheet around market growth plus approximately two percentage points while shifting toward higher-yielding retail and MSME assets.
  • MSME growth was described as below expectation, while management plans to return to scale mode after a cautious quarter.
