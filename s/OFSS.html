Inputs used: ad, eps, group, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

Version: sepa-ratings-1.1. Cohort ranked: 2,019 rows. Rank method: strict-lower empirical percentile, ceil, capped 1–99; ties share a percentile.
