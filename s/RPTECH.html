Weights: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Effective weights on this row: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Basis: full. Missing-input policy: absent components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the row is ranked in the same cohort as full-input rows. SEPA Composite (full-evidence)

SEPA EPS score
