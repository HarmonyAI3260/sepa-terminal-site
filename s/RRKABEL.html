Concall highlights

  • Management reported the highest-ever quarterly revenue, EBITDA and PAT in Q1 FY27.
  • Wires & Cables volumes grew 17% YoY; cables grew more than 25% and wires approximately 12%.
  • Wires & Cables segment margin rose to 9.9% from 7.6%, with scale and cost absorption identified as the largest drivers.
  • Management maintained 10.5% Wires & Cables margin guidance by FY28 and targeted roughly 100 basis points of annual margin improvement.
  • FMEG reached operational breakeven, although management cautioned that seasonally weaker Q2 may not remain at breakeven; full-year breakeven remains the target.
  • FMEG premium products represented approximately 25% of segment revenue, with traction in lighting, appliances and switches.
  • The data-centre opportunity remains at an early stage; a few orders have started, but its current revenue contribution is very small.
  • Management expects approximately 2-3% of the market to shift annually from unorganised to organised participants.
  • The ₹1,200 crore FY26-FY28 Project RRise capex plan remains on track, with around ₹650 crore indicated for FY27.
