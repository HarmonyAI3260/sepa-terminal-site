Catalysts & concall

  • Gold-loan platform expansionQ1 FY27 concall; ANI News headline dated 13 Jul 2026Management expects the Yogloans acquisition, subject to RBI approval, to support roughly 500-plus branches and a ₹4,000-5,000 crore portfolio over three years, adding a secured, potentially higher-ROA growth engine.

  • AUM growth with improving asset qualityQ1 FY27 concallAUM grew 22% YoY while net NPA declined to 0.8% and credit cost fell to 1.0%, showing that growth has not yet required visible deterioration in underwriting outcomes.
  • International funding diversificationQ1 FY27 concallThe US$400 million international bond issue was reportedly four-times subscribed, broadening funding access and supporting balance-sheet capacity.
  • Passive index-related demandSahi headline dated 19 Jun 2026The reported ₹717 crore FTSE-rebalancing inflow may improve institutional visibility and liquidity, though it is a one-off flow rather than a recurring earnings catalyst.

Concall highlights

  • Consolidated AUM reached ₹2.91 lakh crore, up 22% YoY and 5% sequentially; excluding acquired motor finance, AUM growth was 28% YoY.
  • Credit cost declined to 1.0% from 1.6%, net NPA improved sequentially to 0.8%, and consolidated ROA was 2.3%.
  • Retail and SME lending represented 85.4% of the portfolio; management expects the combined share to remain between 85% and 88%.
  • Management targets a 10-basis-point full-year NIM increase and indicated another 3-4 basis points of OPEX benefit by year-end.
  • Gold loans are targeted to reach ₹4,000-5,000 crore of AUM over three years after acquisition, with approximately 500-plus branches.
  • Micro-housing AUM was approximately ₹1,200 crore; management plans 100% growth this financial year and 50-60% the following year, while affordable plus micro housing is targeted to grow around 30%.
  • The US$400 million international bond issue was four-times subscribed, and the company maintained an approximately ₹29,000 crore liquidity buffer.
  • The forming base follows a failed/reset breakout attempt 19 sessions ago and lacks computed setup tightening.
  • RS 74 is adequate but well below elite leadership territory despite positive market-relative performance.
