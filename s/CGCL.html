NOT NOW

CGCL is a confirmed Stage 2 leader with a legacy 3Q consistency test pass and powerful NBFC earnings momentum, but it is 19.9% beyond the supplied pivot, lacks a fresh tight setup, and faces an adverse index regime.

6/10

Guidance / runway

  • Q1 FY27 restored strong acceleration: sales grew 57.0% YoY, PAT 101.7% and EPS 101.6%, with EPS also up 24.8% sequentially. TTM sales grew 50.1% and TTM profit 95.0%. Per concall, AUM grew 62% YoY to ₹40,112 crore, led by gold loans at 111%, housing at 42%, construction finance at 40% and MSME at 24%. Spreads improved to 7.8%, cost-to-income declined to 44.2%, and fee businesses broadened the earnings engine. The key nuance is that growth is being driven partly by rapid balance-sheet and branch expansion, making asset quality and funding execution critical.
  • FY28 guidance raised to ₹65,000 crore AUM

Catalysts & concall

  • FY28 guidance raised to ₹65,000 crore AUMunverifiedManagement guided to 30%+ AUM CAGR through FY28 alongside 19%-21% return on average equity and 4.2%-4.7% return on average assets, creating a measurable growth-and-profitability benchmark.
  • Gold-loan productivity and network expansionunverifiedGold-loan AUM grew 111% YoY, productivity rose to ₹19 crore per branch, and management expected 150-plus additional branches by the end of Q2, supporting further scale and operating leverage.

  • Institutional transaction interestunverifiedCapital Group reportedly bought nearly 49 lakh shares, while a separate ₹250 crore block deal occurred at ₹246. These headlines are consistent with institutional interest but do not by themselves prove sustained accumulation.
