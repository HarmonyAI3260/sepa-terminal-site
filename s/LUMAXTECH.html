Concall highlights

  • Q1 FY27 revenue was ₹1,364 crore, up 33% YoY; EBITDA was ₹205 crore, up 51%, with a 15.1% margin and 190-basis-point expansion.
  • Management expects to sustain the Q1 margin level through the remaining part of FY27 but did not raise overall guidance.
  • The ₹1,600 crore order book is scheduled approximately 24% in FY27, 56% in FY28 and 20% in FY29.
  • Advanced Plastics grew 47%, Mechatronics 56%, Structures and Control Systems 21%, Alternate Fuels 17%, and Aftermarket 6% YoY.
  • Full-year capex guidance remains around ₹300 crore, including Mechatronics and IAC greenfield expansion.
  • Plastics inflation is generally covered through back-to-back OEM pass-through arrangements, often within the same quarter.
  • Management targets roughly 20% revenue CAGR through FY31 and revenue above ₹10,000 crore, with approximately 15% organic growth indicated as attainable even without acquisitions.
  • Five intelligent, connected and software-driven products are planned for launch over 18-24 months; the Body Control Module has already launched.
  • Management expects Mechatronics to reach 14-15% EBITDA margins over the next 12-24 months as joint ventures scale.
  • Aftermarket weakness reflected pricing pressure in non-lighting products, although management retained double-digit growth expectations for the remainder of the year.
