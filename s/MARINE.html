Definitions — how every rating on this page is computed

SEPA Composite (custom)

Percentile (1–99) of 0.35×RS Rating + 0.25×EPS Rating + 0.15×A/D percentile + 0.10×(100 − group-rank percentile) + 0.15×trend points, where trend points = 100×Trend-Template passed/8 in Stage 2 and half of that otherwise. Missing components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the score needs an RS Rating and at least one of the EPS or A/D ratings.

Inputs used: ad, eps, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

Version: sepa-ratings-1.1. Cohort ranked: 2,014 rows. Rank method: strict-lower empirical percentile, ceil, capped 1–99; ties share a percentile. Weights: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Effective weights on this row: ad 0.167 · eps 0.278 · rs 0.389 · trend 0.167. Basis: partial (missing group). Missing-input policy: absent components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the row is ranked in the same cohort as full-input rows. Comparability: partial and full-input composites share one percentile cohort and are not comparable to any commercial rating; the full-evidence composite is the like-for-like figure.

SEPA Composite (full-evidence)

The same weighted mean as the Composite, ranked (1–99) only within the cohort of rows that carry all five components (RS, EPS, A/D, group and trend). A row missing any component is not ranked at all, so this figure is the like-for-like one.

Inputs used: none. Coverage: not ranked: missing group.

Version: sepa-ratings-1.1. Cohort ranked: 327 rows. Rank method: strict-lower empirical percentile, ceil, capped 1–99; ties share a percentile. Comparability: partial and full-input composites share one percentile cohort and are not comparable to any commercial rating; the full-evidence composite is the like-for-like figure.
