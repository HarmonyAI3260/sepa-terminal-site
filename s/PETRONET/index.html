Guidance / runway

  • The EPS/margin beat is real but not volume-led: per concall, Q1 FY27 margin strength came from INR301cr trading gains plus INR193cr inventory gains, both a function of the spot-vs-long-term LNG price disparity created by Qatar's force majeure. Management frames this as an established 'business model' recurring whenever that disparity opens up — but that is an opportunistic/cyclical driver, not organic demand growth. Core throughput (TBTU processed) was down YoY at both Dahej and company level, and the sales decline is compounded by Dahej's nameplate capacity expansion (17.5→22.5 MMTPA from end-March 2026), which mechanically depresses utilization and revenue mix comparisons.
