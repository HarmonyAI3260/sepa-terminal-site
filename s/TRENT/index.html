SEPA Composite (custom)

Inputs used: ad, eps, group, rs, trend. Coverage: complete for this row.

Version: sepa-ratings-1.1. Cohort ranked: 2,122 rows. Rank method: strict-lower empirical percentile, ceil, capped 1–99; ties share a percentile. Weights: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Effective weights on this row: ad 0.15 · eps 0.25 · group 0.1 · rs 0.35 · trend 0.15. Basis: full. Missing-input policy: absent components are dropped and the remaining weights renormalised; the row is ranked in the same cohort as full-input rows. Comparability: partial and full-input composites share one percentile cohort and are not comparable to any commercial rating; the full-evidence composite is the like-for-like figure.

SEPA Composite (full-evidence)
